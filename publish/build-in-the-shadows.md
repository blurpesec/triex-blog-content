# Private Location Sharing - Now with End-to-End Encryption

Trinary Exchange is proud to introduce **Access Control Lists (ACLs) for private location sharing** - giving you granular control over who can see your hub locations in the Trinary. Your position data is protected by end-to-end encryption powered by the [Keyspace](https://github.com/loash-industries/keyspace) project, ensuring that only the characters you explicitly authorize can ever decrypt your coordinates.

## Why Private Location Sharing?

In EVE Frontier, the location of a trading or storage hub is strategic intelligence. Until now, location data was binary: fully public or completely hidden. ACL-based sharing changes that.

With private location sharing, you can:

- **Share selectively** - invite allies to trade or coordinate without broadcasting your position to the entire star map.
- **Revoke instantly** - update your ACL at any time; removed characters immediately lose the ability to decrypt your location data.

## Seamless Marketplace Integration

Private location sharing works natively with the **TriEx Marketplace**. Authorized characters can browse and trade at your private hub just like any public one - the only difference is who gets through the door.

## Security & Privacy

- **True end-to-end encryption** - Location data is encrypted on your device and decrypted only on the devices of authorized characters. TriEx servers never see plaintext coordinates.
- **Immediate revocation** - Remove a character from your ACL and their decryption access is invalidated instantly. They cannot access current or future location updates.

## Get Started

Private location sharing is available now. Click your name in the top-right corner of the TriEx app, then select **"Private Locations"** to get going.

1. **Create an ACL** - Create an access control list and add the characters you want to authorize. Each one receives a cryptographic grant tied to your hub.
2. **Encrypt your location** - Your coordinates are encrypted client-side using Keyspace before being stored. Only authorized characters can decrypt them - no one else, including TriEx servers.
3. **Share securely** - Authorized characters see your hub in the TriEx marketplace and on the star map. Everyone else sees nothing.
