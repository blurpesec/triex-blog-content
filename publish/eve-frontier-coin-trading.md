## Coin Trading Comes to Trinary Exchange

**Items were only the beginning. Trinary Exchange now trades coins.**

---

Until now, every trade on Trinary Exchange was an item changing hands for CRED. Fuel for CRED, ore for CRED, Building Foam for CRED. But CRED isn't the only currency in the Trinary. Players hold EVE, they hold SUI, and some tribes are already thinking about [currencies of their own](https://trinary.exchange/posts/designing-a-tribe-currency?utm_source=blog__coin_trading_announcement&utm_campaign=coin_trading). There was just nowhere to trade them.

**[Currencies](https://trinary.exchange/currencies?utm_source=blog__coin_trading_announcement&utm_campaign=coin_trading)** fixes that. It's a full order-book exchange for coins, built into the same app you already trade items on. Here's what's live.

## Discover Every Coin

Open **Currencies** from the sidebar and you land on **Discover**: every coin listed on Trinary Exchange, priced live in CRED.

![The Discover Coins & Currencies page: EVE at 885.515 CRED with a fixed supply of 10B, CRED at 3.69913B circulating of 10B and mintable, and SUI with a fixed supply](/coin-trading-discover.png)

Each row gives you:

- **Price** - the last traded price in CRED
- **24h change** - which way the market's moving
- **Supply** - circulating supply against total supply, with holdings like treasuries and unactivated balances left out of the circulating figure
- **Contract facts** - whether the coin has a **fixed supply** or is **mintable**, read straight from the chain

That last column matters more than it looks. A coin is only worth trading if you trust its supply, and "the dev said it's capped" isn't the same as the contract saying so. Every fact on this page is verified on-chain, and the **Fixed supply** filter cuts the list down to coins that can never be minted again.

Discover is public. You don't need to log in to see prices.

## Trade Now or Name Your Price

Click any coin to open the trade page. Pick what you're paying with and what you want back, then choose how you want to trade.

**Market** - trade now. Your order fills right away against the best prices on the book. You see an estimate of what you'll receive and how your price compares to the market before you sign anything.

![The Trade page in Market mode: paying 2,000 CRED to receive an estimated 9.64994 EVE at an average of 205 CRED per EVE, marked as filling immediately](/coin-trading-market-order.png)

**Limit** - name your price. Set the most you'll pay (or the least you'll accept) and your order goes on the book for someone else to fill. When it does, you pay the lower maker fee.

![The Trade page in Limit mode: a buy of 2,000 CRED at most 180 CRED per EVE for 10.9902 EVE if fully filled, with a 17.8 CRED fee at 0.90% and the note "Creates an open order for others to fill"](/coin-trading-limit-order.png)

A few things worth knowing:

- **Two markets at launch** - **EVE/CRED** and **SUI/CRED** are open now.
- **Coin-to-coin swaps route through CRED** - Want to go from EVE straight to SUI? Pick both coins and the swap routes through CRED in a single transaction. You don't need to trade twice.
- **Fees are shown up front** - Coin markets launch at 1.10% to take liquidity and 0.90% to make it, and every order shows its fee before you place it. A limit order holds back a small **fee buffer** at the taker rate in case it fills immediately. If it rests on the book instead, the buffer stays in your trading account.
- **Coin trades count toward your fee tier** - Trades run through your trading account, so they build your [fee tier](https://trinary.exchange/posts/trinary-exchange-in-cycle-7?utm_source=blog__coin_trading_announcement&utm_campaign=coin_trading) the same way item trades do. Don't have a trading account yet? Your first trade creates one.
- **Your orders stay in view** - Open limit orders appear right under the trade form, so you can see what's resting without leaving the page.

## Every Order, Every Trade, Out in the Open

Price is one thing. Depth is another. Before you trade size, you want to know who else is in the market and where they're sitting.

**Open Orders** shows every resting buy and sell order across the currency markets, updating live: market, side, price, how much is left, who placed it, and how long it's been there. Filter down to a single coin, or to just the buys or just the sells.

![The Open Orders page filtered to EVE: three resting EVE/CRED orders - sells at 205 and 885.515 CRED and a buy at 175 CRED - with trader names and order age](/coin-trading-open-orders.png)

**Trade History** shows every coin trade that has executed on Trinary Exchange, with price, quantity, and time.

![The Coin Trade History page filtered to EVE: four EVE/CRED trades between 841.240 and 929.790 CRED, with buy or sell side, quantity and time](/coin-trading-history.png)

Both pages are public. Log in and they also pick out your own orders and fills.

## Get Started

Coin trading is live on [trinary.exchange/currencies](https://trinary.exchange/currencies?utm_source=blog__coin_trading_announcement&utm_campaign=coin_trading) now.

1. **Browse the coins** - Open **Currencies** and check prices, supply, and contract facts on the **Discover** tab.
2. **Make your first trade** - Click a coin, pick **Market** to trade now or **Limit** to name your price, and sign.
3. **Watch the book** - Keep an eye on **Open Orders** and **Trade History** to see where the market's heading.
