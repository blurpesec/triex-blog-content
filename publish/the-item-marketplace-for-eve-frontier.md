# Welcome to Trinary Exchange - The Item Marketplace for EVE Frontier

**Trade with anyone, anywhere in the Trinary. Your items, your prices, your rules.**

---

If you've spent any time in EVE Frontier, you know the struggle. You've got so much silica grains in your cargo hold that it's dusting the floor of your cockpit - and somewhere out there, another player is dying for need of silica. **Trinary Exchange changes that.**

We've built the first real player-driven marketplace for EVE Frontier - a place where you can list items for sale, browse what others are offering, and trade directly, all from a single clean interface. No more spamming trade channels. No more coordinating across thousands of light years. Just list, browse, buy, and move on.

## Trade in Public Hubs - or Build Your Own

**Public trade hubs** are open marketplaces where any player can list items and any player can buy them. Think of them as the busy station bazaars of the frontier - drop off your goods, set your price, and let the market come to you. Other players browsing that hub will see your listing, and when they buy, the items move. Simple.

But not every deal needs to be public. Maybe you're running a corp operation and want to move supplies internally. Maybe you've got a trusted circle of allies and you'd rather keep valuable trades within the group. That's where **private trade hubs** come in.

Private hubs work exactly like public ones - list items, set prices, execute trades - but only players you've granted access to can see and buy from them. Your own invite-only marketplace, whether you're stocking a joint operation, running a closed supply chain, or just trading with friends.

## Tribal Shared Storage - Your Crew's Shared Locker

Every player knows the pain of trying to pool resources with their tribe. Someone finds a great mining spot, someone else has the refining skills, a third person has the hauler to move it all - but coordinating who holds what is a nightmare of messages, missed handoffs, and items sitting in the wrong hangar.

**Tribal shared storage** solves this. Your tribe gets a shared vault that any member can deposit into and withdraw from. Found a rare component on a run that you don't need? Drop it in so your tribe-mate can use it for the ship they're building. Need fuel for the next op? Grab it from the stash someone left behind. No begging in tribe chat. No "I'll get it to you next time we're at the same station." The stuff is just there, ready when you need it.

And yes - items in shared storage can be listed on your private trade hub too. The two features work together seamlessly.

## What You Can Do Right Now

Trinary Exchange is live. Here's what's waiting:

- **Browse the markets** - Filter by item type, price, and location across every public hub in the cluster.
- **List your items** - Set your own prices on anything you want to sell. No middleman, no fees eating into your margin.
- **Place orders** - See a buy order you want to fill? Take it. See something you need? Grab it before someone else does.
- **Track your trades** - Full trade history so you always know what you've bought, sold, and where your inventory stands.
- **Manage your inventory** - Everything you've got, across your personal hangar and your tribe's shared vault, in one place.

## Get Started in Three Minutes

1. **Connect your storage** - Trinary Exchange works within your storage unit in the game. Point your storage unit "dapp url" at `trinary.exchange` to get started.
2. **Find a hub** - Browse public hubs near you or set up a private hub for your tribe.
3. **Start trading** - List your first item, browse what's available, make your first deal.

No setup fees, no applications, no waiting.
